### Step-by-Step Generation Method

1. Log in to [Turnitin / Urkund] via institutional credentials at [portal URL].
2. Navigate to your course/assignment folder.
3. Upload the final version of `Paper_Writeup_IEEE.docx` (ensure it is the same version submitted to the instructor).
4. Under *Settings*, enable:
   - Exclude quotes: **ON**
   - Exclude bibliography: **ON**
   - Exclude sources smaller than 5 words: **ON**
5. Submit and wait for the report to generate (typically 10–30 minutes).
6. Open the Similarity Report and record:
   - Overall similarity percentage
   - Breakdown by source type (internet, publications, student papers)
   - Any highlighted passages requiring attention
7. For any highlighted passage >3 consecutive words: review, paraphrase, or add appropriate citation.
8. Re-run if overall similarity exceeds the institutional threshold after corrections.

---

### Interpretation Guide

| Similarity Range | Interpretation | Action Required |
|-----------------|----------------|-----------------|
| 0 – 15% | ✅ Acceptable | No action needed |
| 16 – 24% | ⚠ Borderline | Review highlighted passages; paraphrase or cite |
| 25 – 39% | ❌ Concerning | Significant revision required before submission |
| ≥ 40% | 🚫 Unacceptable | Consult supervisor immediately |

> **Note:** Thresholds vary by institution. Confirm your program's specific policy. Common exception: methodology sections referencing standard equations may show higher similarity due to formulaic language.
